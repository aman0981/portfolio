## 1. Recruiter assessment of your resume

### Where you stand in the market
- **You are already above the median.** AmbitionBox profile data puts Python developers with 2–3 years at roughly ₹4–7 LPA. Your ₹7.5 LPA is above that.
- **A 50% hike requires top-quartile employers.** Hyring puts the median for a Gurugram software engineer at ₹13.4 LPA, with ₹9.1–22.8 LPA for intermediate engineers. ₹11.25 LPA+ is normal at funded product companies, global-capability centres and foreign remote employers. It is not normal at small service firms.
- **NCR jobs titled "web scraping" are mostly low-paid.** Glassdoor showed Gurugram "Web Scraping & Data Extraction Executive" roles at ₹18–30k/month. Scraping work that pays well is mostly **remote** (Wynd Labs, YipitData, Searchlook) or sits inside **backend roles at product companies** (Atlys, GobbleCube). Don't filter on the scraping title alone.
- **Realistic targets:**
  - ₹14–20 LPA from NCR product startups.
  - ₹20–35 LPA from Safe Security, Expedia, Atlys, Ottimate and NextDimension, if you clear their interviews.
  - Anchor at **₹16–18 LPA**. Treat **₹12.5 LPA fixed** as your walk-away floor.

### What sells you, in order of recruiter impact
1. **Kasada and Akamai, named.** For any scraping or integration employer, this is the most credible line on the page. Atlys, GobbleCube, YipitData and Searchlook ask for exactly this.
2. **hirebeacon.in is live and checkable.** It covers 4,959 sources and 69,318 listings, with 336 tests. Most applicants at 2 years of experience have nothing comparable to show.
3. **Go plus Python plus an AWS EKS deployment.** This opens Go roles (Safe Security, Wobot, vCommission, TBO) that a Python-only CV cannot reach.
4. **A promotion within 17 months.** It counters the "3+ years" filter. Mention it in every cover note.

### A compliance note to decide on before applying
Several postings use language about getting past bot defences: GobbleCube ("bypassing Akamai/Cloudflare…"), Searchlook ("fingerprint spoofing") and Datamint ("fingerprint evasion"). Your resume deliberately frames the work as **public data, within terms of service, at polite rate limits**. That framing is accurate and safe, so keep it in interviews. Ask early what the role actually involves, and skip it if the expectations don't match where you draw the line.

At ₹96.30 per US$ (3 Oct 2026, dollarrupee.in), **₹11.25 LPA ≈ $974/month**. As a contractor you need about **$1,150/month** to match it after allowing for benefits you would lose.

| Monthly / annual USD | ≈ INR per year |
|---|---|
| $1,000/mo | ₹11.6L |
| $1,500/mo | ₹17.3L |
| $2,000/mo | ₹23.1L |
| $2,500/mo | ₹28.9L |
| $3,000/mo | ₹34.7L |
| $120k/yr | ₹1.16 Cr |
| $150–200k/yr | ₹1.44–1.93 Cr |

- **EOR (Deel, Remote, Oyster):** you are on the payroll of the provider's Indian entity. You get normal payslips, PF and TDS. Treat it like an Indian job offer.
- **Contractor (likely at Searchlook and Wynd Labs if they hire in India):**
  - You invoice in USD.
  - GST registration applies above ₹20L turnover. Exports are zero-rated under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT).
  - Section 44ADA presumptive tax (50% deemed profit) usually applies to software consulting.
  - You get no PF, gratuity, insurance or paid leave.
  - Confirm the details with a CA before signing.
- **"Remote worldwide" usually comes with a time-zone requirement:**
  - Wynd Labs and Clera: US/EST overlap.
  - Scrapfly: UTC±3, which excludes India.
  - Zyte, Oxylabs, SearchApi and ElevenLabs: India not eligible, so excluded.

### Negotiation
- **The current-CTC question:** give the true figure, ₹7.5 LPA. Indian employers often check payslips or Form 16. Then move the conversation to the role: *"Based on the scope and the market for engineers with production anti-bot extraction, Go and Python, and AWS, I'm looking at ₹16–18 LPA."* Don't negotiate as a percentage hike.
- **Pay structure:** ask for fixed and variable pay separately, and judge offers on fixed pay. ₹12.5L fixed is your floor.
- **Use your notice period:** "I can join in 30 days" is an advantage, so say it early.
- **Timing:** apply in batches so offers land within the same 1–2 weeks. A competing offer is your strongest lever.
